## In one paragraph
Competing inside a category means being compared on its terms. Creating one means defining the terms. Both sources arrive there from different angles: Cole from content, Hormozi from offers.

## What the sources say
- [[library/snow-leopard]] — differentiation comes from a changed point of view, proven on 444 bestsellers.
- [[library/100m-offers]] — niche until the offer is obviously for one person; then price is no longer the comparison.

## My position
Category design is a positioning tool, not a marketing slogan. It works when the new category maps to a real, unserved job; it fails when it is a rename.

## Open questions
- Does this hold for a freelance service, or only for products and content?
