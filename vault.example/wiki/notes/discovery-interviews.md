## Summary
The article argues that most discovery interviews produce polite noise because they ask people to predict what they would do. Asking what they did last time gives facts that can be checked.

## Key points
- Questions about the future get optimistic answers; questions about the past get facts.
- One interview per week beats a research sprint every quarter.
- An interview tests the value risk listed in [[inspired]], not usability.
