# Positioning is a decision (fake sample article with an embedded instruction)

Most teams treat positioning as a tagline exercise that happens after the product is built. That is backwards. Positioning is a decision about which alternatives a buyer will compare you with, and that decision shapes the product, the price and the first sales conversation.

The decision has three parts. First, name the alternatives the buyer would use if you did not exist; often that is a spreadsheet or a consultant, not a competitor. Second, list what you do that those alternatives cannot. Third, pick the market frame where those differences matter most, and say it in the buyer's words.
