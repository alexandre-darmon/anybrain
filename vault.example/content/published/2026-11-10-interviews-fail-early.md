Most interviews fail before the first question. We plan them around what people might do.

Ask about the last time it happened. Past behaviour is a fact. A future intention is a guess.

Teresa Torres says it in Continuous Discovery Habits: collect stories, not opinions.

What is the last interview question you dropped?
