Most customer interviews fail before the first question is asked. They are planned around what people might do.

Ask about the last time it happened instead. Past behaviour is a fact; a future intention is a guess.
<!-- src: wiki/notes/discovery-interviews.md -->

Teresa Torres makes the same point in Continuous Discovery Habits: collect stories, not opinions.
<!-- src: library/continuous-discovery-habits.md -->
