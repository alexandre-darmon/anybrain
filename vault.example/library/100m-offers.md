## My take
Repetitive, but the value equation is worth the whole book.

## Key ideas
- Value is dream outcome times likelihood, divided by time and effort. Every term can be worked on.
- Niche down until the offer is obvious to one person; this is the offer-side version of [[category-design]].
- A named offer is easier to want and to repeat, an application of [[languaging]].
- Price is compared only when the offer looks like the alternatives.
- Guarantees reduce perceived risk more than discounts reduce price.

## Quotes
- "The value equation has four levers, and most offers pull only one." (loc. 233)
