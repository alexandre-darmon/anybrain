## My take
Best book I read on differentiation. The analysis of 444 bestsellers is the proof, not the theory.

## Key ideas
- Being different is being a snow leopard: rare in a niche, not better in a crowd. This is the core argument behind [[category-design]].
- Obvious creators sell information; non-obvious creators sell experience.
- The reader is the main character: write their transformation, not your expertise.
- Naming a new concept separates it from the old ones, which is the practice described in [[languaging]].
- A category is defined by its point of view, so the same topic can hold several categories.

## Quotes
- "Obvious creators sell information. Non-obvious creators sell experience." (loc. 412)
- "Languaging is the act of naming the new so that it can never be confused with the old." (loc. 530)
