## My take
Best book I read on differentiation. The analysis of 444 bestsellers is the proof, not the theory.

## Key ideas
- Being different is being a snow leopard: rare in a niche, not better in a crowd. → [[wiki/concepts/category-design]]
- Obvious creators sell information; non-obvious creators sell experience.
- The reader is the main character; write their transformation, not your expertise.
- "Languaging": naming a new concept separates it from the old ones. → [[wiki/concepts/languaging]]
